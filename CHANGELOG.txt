EdgePress Changelog
===================

2026.9.6 — 2026-09-25
---------------------

- Added a shared, locally hosted SVG icon library with 22 reusable icons. Theme navigation uses common icons, and feature-grid cards can opt into named icons.
- Added the EdgePress page mark, folded-paper sparrow mascot, and a 1200 × 630 Open Graph image. Themes include the mark as their favicon and show the mascot on the homepage.
- Added accessible older/newer archive navigation for paginated homepage post lists.
- Added localized post author metadata, visible bylines, and matching BlogPosting structured data.
- Removed stale demo-video references from page and post content. No video assets are generated by this release.
- Kept icons, branding, and share assets local, with no new runtime dependencies or third-party asset requests.
