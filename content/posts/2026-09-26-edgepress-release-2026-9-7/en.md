This is the seventh requested EdgePress update for September 2026.

## Consent and privacy disclosures

Cloudflare Web Analytics is configured as a statistics service and remains behind the visitor's explicit consent. Browser service groups now live under `plugins.consent` in `config.yml`. The privacy policy lists each enabled service's provider, category, purpose, and retention period when the site is built.

## Images and article lists

The homepage uses smaller responsive mascot images. Article archives are sorted newest-first across all available languages, while homepage latest-post lists stay in the current language. Post excerpts now remove Markdown syntax before display.

## Mobile controls and maintenance

The consent panel has a responsive mobile layout, clearer service details, and larger touch targets. The unused integrations directory and obsolete cleanup paths for retired audit files have been removed.
