EdgePress now connects to CommentNest · 评巢 using a configured backend URL. The independent project owns the complete comment widget, translations, styling, stickers and GitHub App backend. The framework keeps the consent gate and published article/page context.

Privacy choices survive refresh and navigation, including browsers where persistent local storage is unavailable. Cosmetic names and translations no longer reset saved choices. Changing services, endpoints or notice dates asks for a new choice.

[Install CommentNest and connect EdgePress](https://github.com/jsw-teams/CommentNest/blob/main/docs/edgepress.md).
